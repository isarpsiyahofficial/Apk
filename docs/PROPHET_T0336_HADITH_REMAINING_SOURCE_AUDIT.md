# T0336 Hadith Remaining Source Audit

After batches 32–39, twenty of the twenty-five canonical prophets have an admitted biography-owned hadith source. Five remain deliberately unresolved: **Hûd, Şuayb, Zülkifl, İlyâs, Elyesa‘**.

This is a fail-closed editorial decision, not an assertion that no narration can exist anywhere. The release gate requires an exact, source-reviewed Sahih/Hasan report whose proposition can safely be owned by the biography subject. Search hits, later qisas literature, Israiliyyat, a similarly named person, or an indirect community/event report are insufficient.

## Reviewed unresolved cases

- **Hûd** — TDV's *HÛD* article establishes the Quranic identity and records differing later genealogical traditions. The current T0336 review did not locate an exact primary Sahih/Hasan locator that directly makes a biography-owned proposition about Hûd. Reports merely describing the destruction of ʿÂd are not automatically Hûd-owned evidence.
- **Şuayb** — TDV's *ŞUAYB* article records substantial disagreement over genealogy, chronology and the identification of Mûsâ's Midianite host. It secondarily points to a report concerning Arab prophets, but the T0336 pass has not established an exact primary Sahih/Hasan locator sufficient for direct Şuayb ownership. The slot therefore remains unresolved.
- **Zülkifl** — TDV's *ZÜLKİFL* article explicitly distinguishes the Quranic Zülkifl from the man called **Kifl** in a report transmitted by Ahmad/Tirmidhi, describes that report as *gharīb*, and states that the identification cannot be made. A Kifl report must therefore fail closed rather than verify Zülkifl.
- **İlyâs** — TDV's *İLYÂS* article separates the Quranic account from extensive later material derived largely from Jewish/Israelite traditions. It also records scholarly rejection of fabricated claims that İlyâs remains alive. Those narratives are not admissible as direct hadith evidence for the biography gate.
- **Elyesa‘** — TDV's *ELYESA‘* article states that apart from two Quranic mentions and genealogy supplied in Islamic sources, no further information is available. The application must preserve that uncertainty instead of manufacturing a hadith slot.

## Release consequence

The hadith dimension is currently **20/25 verified, 5/25 explicit unresolved**. These five records must stay unresolved until exact primary-source locator, grading/source class and semantic ownership are all independently verified. Their unresolved status must not be converted into family/lineage, geography, chronology or historical-date evidence.
