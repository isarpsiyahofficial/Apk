# T0336 Hadith Batch 38 — İsmâil, Sâlih, Zekeriyyâ

This batch advances only the `hadith` dimension of the mandatory 25×8 prophet semantic release gate. It does **not** promote hadith content into lineage, geography, chronology, or historical-date coverage.

## Admitted references

- **İsmâil — Sahih al-Bukhari 3364** (`REFERENCE-ONLY`): the report explicitly narrates İsmâil with Hâcer at Zamzam and later İsmâil assisting İbrâhim while raising the Ka'ba foundations. TDV's *Zemzem* article independently cites Bukhârî, Enbiyâ 9 for the Hâcer/İsmâil/Zamzam account.
- **Sâlih — Sahih al-Bukhari 3377** (`REFERENCE-ONLY`): the report explicitly identifies the she-camel as belonging to Prophet Sâlih and discusses the person who hamstrung it. TDV's *Sâlih* article and Diyanet's Qur'an Yolu material independently preserve the Sâlih–Semûd–she-camel context while warning against unsupported narrative embellishment.
- **Zekeriyyâ — Sahih Muslim 2379** (`REFERENCE-ONLY`): the report explicitly states that Zekeriyyâ was a carpenter. TDV's *Zekeriyyâ* article cites Muslim, Fezâil 169 for this fact; Diyanet's *Hadislerle İslâm* volume 6 gives the same Muslim reference.

## Ownership / failure-path policy

- Each claim is biography-owned and subject-owned by the same prophet.
- Natural cross-mentions (for example İbrâhim inside the İsmâil report) are context only and do not transfer ownership.
- Source id/title/class/license/locator are exact metadata; tampering fails closed.
- These records cannot by themselves verify family/lineage, chronology, geography, or exact historical dates.

## Remaining hadith owners after this batch

Seven canonical prophets remain explicit unresolved for the hadith dimension: `hud`, `ishaq`, `yakub`, `shuayb`, `dhul-kifl`, `ilyas`, `alyasa`. They must remain unresolved unless a suitable source-reviewed hadith can be admitted without semantic overreach.
